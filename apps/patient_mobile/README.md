## EazyClinic patient application (Phase 8)

Real, API-integrated screens: account setup, home, clinics and linking, doctors, booking, appointments with check-in and queue status, records (overview, released visit summaries, prescriptions), bills and receipts, family, notifications, privacy and consent, profile. Architecture and rules: `docs/phase-8/`. Build with `--dart-define-from-file=../../environments/<env>/app.json` (only `*.example.*` templates are committed). Push delivery, documents and reports, online payment and release signing are not done; see `docs/phase-8/PATIENT_ANDROID_RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md`.
